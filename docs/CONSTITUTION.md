# Draft Constitution: 0x🟨 Road Public Utility

*Design direction, not a community-ratified charter.*

## 1. Free participation, gas-paid on-chain registration

IP works and metadata can exist off-chain without payment. An originator who activates a contract on an EVM chain pays normal blockchain gas. There is no Road surcharge for brick registration. Originators do **not** need to own blockchain nodes to register IP or receive royalties.

## 2. Services, not speculation

The Road is a public utility funded by use, not by investment sales, token speculation, yield or speculative node purchases. License compensation and creator royalty payments are independent of network operating fees. Only operators who **own/operate qualifying actual blockchain nodes** and perform verifiable Road infrastructure work can receive Road utility fees. Owning a Yellow Brick is not owning a blockchain node. There is no passive compensation merely for having a node. Smart contracts alone cannot prove physical ownership or operation of node hardware; challenges and external attestations are needed.

## 3. Collection capped by public budget

Commercial protocol settlement may carry a disclosed microfee. The total collectible amount for a funding period is capped by a published, democratically approved operating budget plus approved reserves, minus unspent unrestricted funds and other funding. At each eligible payment: `fee = min(floor(payment * approvedBps / 10000), remainingCap)`. At cap, the Road collects zero additional fees while normal settlements can continue. The fee does not control underlying blockchain gas. Budget adjustments are prospective, approved, timelocked, and audited; simple period resets cannot evade caps.

## 4. Every verified user votes, weighted

Every genuinely distinct Road participant receives a baseline governance voice. Additional weight can reward provable contributions by creators, users, developers and node operators, within limits. Governance votes must not be purchasable by wealth, transaction size, number of wallets, number of IP objects or multiple related nodes. Personhood / Sybil resistance, the exact formula, quorum, delegation and high-impact approval rules remain open. A blockchain wallet is not necessarily a person.

## 5. Limits on governance

Governance may maintain standards and prospectively approve network fees, cap changes, operator qualifications and public budgets. It cannot seize an IP, declare a legal copyright winner, retroactively rewrite a creator's grant, redirect royalties or falsify provenance. Public dispute and appeal procedures must be developed.

## 6. Transparency

Budget approvals, operating payouts, receipts, controls and governance actions should be visible and auditable. Private creative content must not be needlessly revealed in order to register its commitments.
